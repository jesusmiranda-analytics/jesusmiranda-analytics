## About

I am a **Data Analyst and Business Intelligence professional in training** focused on turning complex data into clear, actionable business decisions. My work spans data preparation, KPI design, executive dashboards, statistical analysis, predictive modeling, machine learning, CRM analytics, and process automation.

As a dual-degree student in **Marketing and Business Intelligence at Tecnológico de Monterrey**, I combine quantitative analysis with commercial and strategic context. My approach is to understand the decision first, build a trustworthy analytical process, validate the evidence, and communicate insights in a way that stakeholders can act on.

- **Data Analysis & BI:** Python, SQL, R, MySQL, PostgreSQL, Power BI, Tableau, Looker, SPSS, KPI frameworks, and executive reporting.
- **AI / ML:** supervised learning, ensemble models, probability calibration, clustering, forecasting, anomaly detection, and human-in-the-loop analysis.
- **Business Analytics:** CRM and customer analytics, supply-chain KPIs, service-quality diagnostics, segmentation, CLV, and strategic recommendations.
- **Technical Delivery:** reproducible data pipelines, ETL automation, data validation, APIs, dashboards, and documented end-to-end analytical workflows.

### Open To

Data Analyst, Business Intelligence Analyst, Junior Data Scientist, AI/ML Analyst, analytics consulting, and high-impact projects connecting data with business strategy.

## Featured Projects

<details open>
<summary><strong>CDD-Mundial — FIFA World Cup 2026 Forecasting System</strong></summary>

<br>

An end-to-end data science system that forecasts the 2026 FIFA World Cup with dynamic Elo ratings, Dixon–Coles modeling, calibrated XGBoost, and tournament-wide Monte Carlo simulation.

| Dimension | Details |
|:---|:---|
| **Stack** | Python · NumPy · pandas · XGBoost · Pandera · Pytest · Matplotlib |
| **Scale** | 48 teams · 104 fixtures · 49,000+ historical matches · thousands of tournament simulations |
| **Performance** | Temporal holdouts, log-loss, Brier/RPS, calibration gates, vectorized simulation |
| **Security** | Gitignored secrets, restricted-source policy, automated secret scanning, immutable raw captures |
| **Impact** | Reproducible live snapshots with full data/model lineage and promotion gates |
| **Repository** | [View source](https://github.com/jesusmiranda-analytics/Data-Science---World-Cup) |

The system uses strict, coercing Pandera contracts, canonical team identities, deterministic seeds, SHA-256 provenance manifests, and append-only production snapshots. Its ML upgrade is promoted only when it beats the structural baseline on out-of-sample calibration—not because a model is more complex.

</details>
